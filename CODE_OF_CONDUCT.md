# DiagEvidence code of conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome questions and
give specific, evidence-based technical feedback. Different experience levels
and research backgrounds are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing another
person's private information and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.
Critique code and claims rather than people. Respect requests to end unwanted
interaction.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed spaces. The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content,
close a discussion or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate
to the behavior and consider context and previous incidents.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. Include links or a factual description and state whether
your report may be shared. Do not post sensitive personal information in a
public issue. If no private contact method is available, a public issue may ask
the maintainer for a private reporting channel without identifying the people
or details involved.
